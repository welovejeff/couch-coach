Paper and print

In the print window, check these

    To save a file instead, press Print and choose “Save as PDF” as the printer. On an iPhone, pinch the preview open, then tap Share.

    My printer can't print two-sided

    Print page 1 only. Put the sheet back in the tray with the blank side ready, then print page 2 only. Or choose “One side” above and print a single page.

    Make it last

    Card stock or a plastic sleeve lasts longer than plain paper. Use a dry-wipe pen on a sleeve and the stars can be wiped and colored again.

    No printer near this device? This makes a link that opens the same sheet somewhere else. It carries your set-up, colors and builds, never the name.
