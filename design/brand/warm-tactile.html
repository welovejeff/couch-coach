Couch Coach · brand route

Warm and tactile

The brand is the foam pieces themselves: three soft shapes in three colors on a warm floor, with words a tired parent can take in at a glance.

1Logo

A slab, a triangle pillow and a hoop, stacked the way a kid would stack them. Together they read as a couch seen from the side. The wordmark is drawn from one thick, round-ended line, like a foam tube bent into letters.

Stacked, for square spaces. The letters line up: only the u and the a differ.

The idea in one line: the three shapes in the mark are the three kinds of build, so the logo, the filters and the printed sheet all speak with the same three pieces.

4Voice and copy

Talk like the parent on the next cushion: short, plain and specific, with no exclamation marks. Say what a thing is and what to do next, admit what has not been tried, and leave the excitement to the kid.

6What this route is not

  • Not a nursery.No pastel-on-white, no baby talk, no mascot with eyes. A parent should be happy to leave it open on their phone.
  • Not a toy aisle.No rainbow of primaries, no bursts, badges or exclamation marks. Three colors, used the same way every time.
  • Not an app dashboard.No gradients, glass or thin grey type. Edges are solid and thick, like a slab of foam seen from the side.
