Couch Coach · brand route

Calm and minimal

A tidy playroom. Warm paper, soft foam colours, one quiet serif, and a lot of room around the drawings so a parent can find a build with one thumb and a child can point at the one they want.

Climb and slide

6 · What this route is not

Keeping it distinct

  • Not loud.No primary colours, no thick coloured borders, no emoji as decoration. If a screen feels busy, something comes off it.
  • Not a toy catalogue.No characters, mascots or cartoon lettering. The builds are the only pictures.
  • Not cold.Warm paper, not white and grey. A serif with some softness, not a tech sans. It should feel like a home, not an app.
