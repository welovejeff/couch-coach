Naming check: Couch Coach

Should the site keep its working name? Eight alternatives were tested against it. Domains were checked on 18 November 2026.

Recommendation: keep Couch Coach. None of the alternatives is clearly better: the descriptive ones are easier to find but forgettable, and the playful ones each cover only part of the library or drop the word "couch". Register couchcoach.com, also take playcouchbuilds.com as a redirect, and always show the name with the line "build ideas for your play couch" so nobody reads it as fitness or life coaching.

How Couch Coach holds up

Said aloud in a video
Two short words, same first and last sound, easy to remember. The weak point is that only the vowel differs, so said quickly it can blur into "coach coach" or "couch couch". Say it slowly once and put it on screen as text in every video.
Spelling after one hearing
Good. Both are common words with one usual spelling, and there is no "and", "&", number or hyphen to get wrong. The domain is the name with nothing added.
What it promises
Someone who shows you what to do with the couch. That fits the step-by-step build view well. It does not say "ideas" or "builds", and it does not say "play", so the name needs its descriptor line beside it.
How a child hears it
Fine but neutral. A 2 to 5 year old can say it, and it sounds a little silly, which helps. "Coach" means little to them, so the child-facing warmth has to come from the drawings and a mascot or mark, not the word.
Possible confusion
Three, all mild: fitness ("couch to 5K" style programmes), life coaching or therapy (the couch as the therapist's couch), and the armchair sports fan or "couch potato". With a picture of a foam fort next to it, none of these survive more than a second. Without a picture, in a search result or a text link, the fitness reading is the likely first guess.
Trademark distance
Uses no play-couch brand name. "Couch" and "coach" are ordinary words. A proper trademark search for "Couch Coach" has not been done and should be before printing anything or selling anything under the name.

Also checked and ruled out

  • .com already taken: cushionclub.com, couchquest.com, couchcrew.com, stackaroo.com, foamfort.com, fortbook.com, todaywebuild.com, fortling.com, fortsy.com, playcouchclub.com.
  • Available but weaker: Wobblefort (sounds unsafe), Fort Menu (forts only, and "menu" suggests food), Stack and Flop ("and" or "&"), Couch Craft (crafting, not building), Pillow Pilot (pillows are not the product), Cushion Coach (no gain over the current name).
  • Still available from the earlier check: playcouchideas.com and couchbuilds.com. Either works as a second redirect; neither is needed.

If the name stays

  • Lock the descriptor: Couch Coach: build ideas for your play couch. Use it in the page title, the header and every video caption.
  • Let the logo do the disambiguating. A mark made from the foam shapes removes the fitness reading faster than any wording.
  • Register couchcoach.com now and point playcouchbuilds.com at it. The second one catches people who search by description.
  • Check social handles for "couchcoach" before committing. That has not been done here.

How the domains were checked: GoDaddy availability lookup, exact .com match only, 18 November 2026. "Available" means it could be registered at the time of the check; no prices were returned. Trademark registers and social handles were not checked for any name.
