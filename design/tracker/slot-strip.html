Design notes · Slot strip

The idea

The tracker is the paper itself, laid flat: six places per page, each one filling with a tiny drawing of the build as it is picked. Empty places show where the next pick lands, so "how far along am I" needs no reading.

One page or two

Every sheet starts as one page of six. When page one is full the outline goes solid, a check appears, and page two is offered: as ghost slots beside it on a wide screen, as a "+ Page 2" tile and an "Add page two" button on a phone. Picking a seventh build also starts page two, so nobody hits a wall. On a phone one page shows at a time and the other folds into a small tile you tap to flip. An empty page two can be removed again.

States

  • Nothing picked: no tracker at all.
  • First pick: strip appears, slot 1 filled, "+" in slot 2. Button: "Open sheet".
  • Page one full: solid outline and check. Buttons: "Add page two" and "Open 1-page sheet".
  • Working on page two: "8 of 12 picked". Button: "Open 2-page sheet".
  • All full: "Both pages are full". A 13th tap says to take one off first.

Suggestions, removing, hiding, clearing

  • Suggestions are left out of the strip. It shows picks only, and one line says the empty places get suggestions when the sheet opens.
  • Remove: tap a slot to select it. Its name appears with "Take off" and "Keep". The place stays open and the next pick fills it. The card's own button still works too.
  • Hide: tucks the tracker into a "Show sheet · 3 of 6" pill at the bottom right. The pill keeps counting and brings it back in one tap.
  • Clear: asks first ("Take all 7 builds off the sheet?"), then offers Undo for 8 seconds.

Mobbin references

  • Snapchat, photo multi-select: a thumbnail strip in pick order beside one forward button. Screen
  • Shopee, media picker: the limit said in plain words right above the thumbnails. Screen
  • Shopify, select files: "3 selected" as the headline of a bottom panel of thumbnails. Screen
  • Crumbl Cookies, box builder: the button's words change with how many are left. Screen
  • Fable, "Pick 5-8": a range, not one fixed number. Six is enough, twelve is the most. Screen
