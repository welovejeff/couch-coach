Print a sheet
Design notes: Star path Mock-up. Nothing is saved.

The idea

The printed sheet gives the child a star to color for each build they make, so the tracker is that same row of six stars: each build you add colors one in, left to right, in its theme color with its theme shape inside. Fill six and the front is done; stop there, or turn the row over and fill six more for the back.

States

  • Empty: no tracker at all. It appears with the first pick.
  • First pick to five: "Max's sheet: 3 of 6". Colored stars are picks, outlined stars are open. One button: Open sheet. Open spots are filled with ideas that fit, so the sheet always prints full.
  • Front full: the stars do one small wave and the slab, the triangle and the hoop look over the edge of the tray, then duck back. Two buttons: Add a back page, Open 1-page sheet.
  • Working on the back: the row turns over to a fresh six. "Back: 3 of 6". Buttons: See the front, Open 2-page sheet. From 900px wide both rows show side by side and nothing flips.
  • All full: "Both sides are full". A thirteenth tap changes nothing and the tray says "Take one off to add another."

One page or two

  • A sheet starts as one page. The choice is only asked when it matters: at six stars.
  • Add a back page turns the row over. Tapping "Add to sheet" on a seventh card does the same thing, so a parent on a roll is never stopped.
  • The button always says what will open: Open sheet, Open 1-page sheet, Open 2-page sheet.
  • To go back to one page: Clear, then Remove the back page.

Taking picks off, hiding, clearing

  • One pick: tap its card button again, or tap its star to see its name, then Take it off. Later stars slide down so the row never has gaps.
  • Hide: tucks the tray into a small pill at the bottom right ("3 of 6 · Show") that stays for as long as picks exist. New picks bump the pill; it does not spring back open, except to explain a full sheet.
  • Clear: asks first, with Keep them as the main button. After clearing, a "Sheet cleared. Undo" bar stays until it is dismissed or a new pick is made.

Suggestions and theme shapes

  • Suggestions are no longer counted in the tracker. They are one quiet line: "Pick 3 more, or we fill the rest for you."
  • Theme colors are used only as themes: a star takes the color and shape of its build's theme, so the row also shows the mix (all forts, or a bit of everything).

References (Mobbin)

  • Honest Greens, stamp card: stamped spots beside numbered empty ones on a fixed-size card. Borrowed: a fixed row that fills in order, and a second card behind the first.
  • Finch, streak row and "A Shiny Star for You": a calm seven-spot strip and a soft reward moment. Borrowed: the tone of the page-full moment.
  • Duolingo ABC and Uxcel, earned stars: colored star against a pale outline. Borrowed: filled against outlined as the whole progress language, no numbers needed for a child.
  • Rocket Money and Shopee, selection bars: "Continue with 2 Subscriptions", "Add (3)". Borrowed: the button itself says what you get (Open 2-page sheet).
  • pushr, week widget: a compact row with the count beside it. Borrowed: title and count on one line above the row.
