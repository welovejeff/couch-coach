Design notes Mock-up C: Mini paper

The idea. The tracker is a tiny picture of the thing being made: one or two little pages, each two across and three down, that fill with color as builds are picked. Tap it and it pulls up into a bigger view of the same paper, where picks can be moved or taken off and the parent chooses 1 page or 2.

States

  • Empty. Nothing picked, no tracker. It appears on the first pick, as it does today.
  • First pick. One page. The first spot turns the build's theme color; the other five are pale, because we fill them with suggestions. A blank second page peeks out from behind. Button: Open sheet.
  • Page one full. Six solid spots. The peeking page shows a plus. "Page one is full. Pick one more to start page two." Button becomes solid: Print 1 page.
  • Working on page two. The seventh pick slides the second page out. "7 of 12 picked. Page two started." Button goes back to Open sheet.
  • All full. Twelve solid spots. "All 12 picked. Both pages are full." Button: Print 2 pages. A thirteenth tap changes nothing and says "Take one off to add another."
  • Expanded. Tap the tracker, or swipe it up. Close with the handle, a swipe down, Keep picking, the dimmed page or Escape. Tap a build on the paper, then act on it in the bar below: Earlier, Later, Take off, or for a suggestion, Keep it and Show another.
  • Hidden. The Hide tab on the tracker's top edge (or a swipe down) tucks it away. A small tile stays in the bottom corner with the mini paper, the count and Show. It keeps counting while tucked and never comes back by itself.
  • Cleared. Clear sheet lives only in the pull-up. It asks first ("Take all 7 picks off the sheet?"), and after it clears a bar offers Undo for ten seconds.

1 page or 2 pages

  • A sheet starts as 1 page (6 builds). If the parent just keeps picking, the seventh pick starts page two. Nobody has to decide up front.
  • The pull-up has the plain choice: "1 page, 6 builds" or "2 pages, 12 builds". The dashed second page on the desk is the same choice as a big tap target.
  • Once the parent chooses 1 page, it stays 1 page: a seventh pick is refused with "Page is full. Take one off, or add page two."
  • Going from 2 pages to 1 with more than six picks asks first, takes the last picks off, and offers Undo.
  • Suggestions are ghosted, not empty: pale color on the mini paper, a faded drawing with a dashed edge in the pull-up. The printed sheet is never half blank.
  • Picks sit first, in the order they were picked; suggestions fill the spots after them.

References from Mobbin

  • Apple Music and Spotify, now playing: a small bar that grows into a full view, with a grab handle on top and a swipe down to put it back.
  • Snapchat and Shopee, media pickers: a bottom tray that shows what was picked, in order, beside one button, with the limit said in plain words.
  • Google Photos, photo book "Edit page": tap a thing on the page, then act on it in a bar underneath (Remove, Replace).
  • Polarsteps, travel book: pages drawn as paper, a plus between pages, and the page count beside the main button.
  • WHOOP and Temu, cart sheets: progress toward a goal on top, "Keep shopping" to close, and removing everything kept inside the sheet, away from the bar.
