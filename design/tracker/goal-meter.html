Design notes: Goal meter mock-up B of the sheet tracker

The idea. The tracker is one slim bar of six spots with a milestone at the end, like a free-shipping meter, and one line that says what the next pick gets you. At six the goal is reached and the bar offers one choice: print a one-page sheet, or keep going and the bar grows to twelve.

States

  • Empty: no tracker at all. It appears with the first pick.
  • First picks (1 to 5): "3 of 6 picked. 3 more fills page one." The button is a quiet "Open sheet".
  • Page one full (6): "Page one is full." Two buttons: "Print 1 page" (solid) and "Fill the back".
  • Working on page two (7 to 11): the bar is twelve spots with a 6 marker in the middle. "9 of 12 picked. 3 more fills the back."
  • All full (12): "Both sides are full." The button is a solid "Print 2 pages". A 13th tap changes nothing and the line says to take one off first.

One page or two

  • A sheet starts as one page. It becomes two when you tap "Fill the back" or simply pick a 7th build. The 7th pick is never refused; a line says "Started the back of the sheet" with Undo.
  • To go back: take picks off until six or fewer are left (a back that was started by picking closes by itself), or open the tracker and choose "1 page". If that drops picks, they are named in a line with Undo.
  • Suggestions: spots you have not picked are filled with our suggestions when the sheet opens, as today. They show as hollow spots on the bar and never count as progress. A checkbox in the panel turns them off, so only your picks print.
  • The name: the bar itself stays nameless so it stays short. The panel heading carries it ("Cole and Thomas's sheet") and wraps onto a second line when it is long.
  • Taking a pick off: tap "On your sheet" on the card again, or open the tracker (tap the line) and tap "Off" beside the build.
  • Filled spots take the color of each build's theme, so the bar also shows the mix. Ink stays the only tap color.

Hide and clear

  • Hide: the "Hide" link on the bar tucks the tracker into a small pill at the bottom right that keeps counting ("Sheet: 3 of 6"). Tap the pill to bring it back. It stays tucked while you pick.
  • Clear sheet: lives inside the panel, not on the bar, so it takes two taps to reach. It asks first ("Take all 9 builds off the sheet?"), and after clearing the bar stays for a few seconds with "Sheet cleared. Undo".

References (Mobbin)

  • SHEIN cart: a pinned bottom strip with one sentence, a thin bar and one button. This is the layout.
  • WHOOP cart: "Spend $6 more to get free shipping" over a bar, then Checkout and Keep shopping. This is the wording pattern and the two-button choice at the goal.
  • Instacart cart: "Add $7.33 to get $0 delivery fee" on a hairline bar. Proof the meter can be very slim. Its greyed-out checkout button is what we chose not to copy: our button always works.
  • Nike Run Club levels: a bar split by a tick with a marker at the next milestone. This is the 6 and 12 markers.
  • Google Photos and Apple News selection mode: a count with a clear or trash control beside it. This is why clearing sits next to the count, but behind a confirm.
