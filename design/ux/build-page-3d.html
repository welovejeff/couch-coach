What changed and why (build page and 3D view)
  1. The picture shows at once; 3D is a button. Today the page shows "Loading 3D view…" until about 2 MB of 3D code arrives, and if 3D fails the step-through is removed. Here the flat drawing and every step work with no 3D at all.
  2. Each step says what to do. Today: "Step 3 of 8: Triangle pillow". Here: "Stand a triangle pillow on its end on the floor." The new piece is in full colour, earlier pieces are faded, and the picture never jumps size between steps.
  3. One big Next button at the bottom of the phone. Today four small buttons sit mid-page and the darkest one is "Show all". Here Next fills the thumb area and stays on screen while you scroll.
  4. A parts list before you start. What the build needs, how much floor it takes, what is left over, and whether your set-up has it. Rows tick off as pieces go down.
  5. 3D no longer traps the page. Today the 3D box fills 58% of the screen and a thumb on it spins the model instead of scrolling. Here 3D is opt-in and one tap returns to the picture.
  6. All steps on one screen, and "Print this build". One Letter page: the finished picture, the parts, and every step. Try it with the Print button.
  7. Somewhere to go when you finish. Today the only way on is back to the wall of 36. Here the page ends with builds that use the same pieces, plus previous and next.
  8. "Not yet kid-tested" stays put and explains itself. Next to the name, on every card, on the printout. Tap it for one plain sentence.
