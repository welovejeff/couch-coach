What changed and why

  1. You can add a build to the sheet from where you find it.Today the only way in is a small "Printable sheet" text link, and the sheet ignores what you liked. Gallery cards get an "Add to sheet" button and a running count.
  2. The paper is the editor.Today the 12 builds are fixed. Tap any card on the preview to swap it, keep it, or move it. "Shuffle" changes every card you have not kept.
  3. The sheet uses every build you can make.Today a family with a round add-on never sees the 12 couch-only builds on a sheet. Now swaps draw from all builds that fit what you own.
  4. Choices that match real printers and real toddlers.A4 or US Letter (guessed from your region), one side or two, 6 big or 4 bigger pictures, and a less-ink or color-in version.
  5. Print settings shown as a short list that follows your choices.Today it is one dense paragraph that only fits US Letter two-sided. Now four lines, plus help for printers that cannot print two-sided.
  6. A way out for a phone with no printer.Most visitors arrive on a phone. "Send to my computer" shares a link that reopens this exact sheet. Nothing is uploaded; the choices travel in the link.
  7. A star a child can actually color in.Today the star is already filled and half an inch wide. It is now a larger empty outline, numbers are bigger, and the child's name is on both sides.
  8. The controls stop covering the page on a phone.Today a sticky toolbar takes about a third of a phone screen. Settings scroll away; only a one-line bar with Print stays.

    My printer can't print two-sided

    Print page 1 only. Put the sheet back in the tray with the blank side ready, then print page 2 only. Or choose "One side" above and print a single page.

    Make it last

    Card stock or a plastic sleeve survives a toddler longer than plain paper. Use a dry-wipe pen on a sleeve and the stars can be wiped and colored again.
