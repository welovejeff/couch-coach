What changed and why (design note, not part of the page)
  1. The headline says what this is and how much there is. "36 build ideas for your play couch" replaces a logo plus a grey sentence. The product-fit line sits under the picker, where the question "does this fit mine?" comes up.
  2. "What do you own?" is four pictures, not four text chips. A parent taps the picture that looks like their living room. It adds a fourth choice the site lacks today: 2 couches + round add-on (that family can make all 36, but today no chip lets them say so).
  3. Before you answer, you still see all 36, grouped by what each needs, smallest set-up first. Nobody is stopped by a question, and the first pictures on screen are builds everyone can make.
  4. After you answer, builds you cannot make do not vanish. They fold into one line at the bottom ("12 more need a second play couch"). Builds that use everything you own come first.
  5. Every card carries a small set-up tag ("1 couch", "1 couch + add-on", "2 couches") next to the theme. The card now answers "can I make this?" without opening it.
  6. "Not yet kid-tested" stays on every card, moved from a stamp over the picture to a line under the name, and explained once at the bottom of the page.
  7. Theme filter sticks to the top while you scroll, with a count of what is showing and a "Change set-up" link. Today the filters scroll away after the first row.
  8. Couch colours are swatches, one tap each, behind a "Match my colours" link beside the picker. The pictures, including the four picker pictures, recolour at once.
  9. A video link can name its build. Adding ?seen=race-car to the address puts that build first, labelled "From the video".
