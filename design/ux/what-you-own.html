What changed and why

Design sprint track: what-you-own set-up. Styling is neutral on purpose; a separate track owns the brand. Everything below the notes is live: pick, recolour, switch pages.

  1. Ask with pictures, not words. Today the question is four text chips. Here it is four drawings of the actual pieces, in your colours, each with how many builds it opens. One tap answers it.
  2. Ask on the page, never as a gate. The gallery is still the home page and shows all 36 builds until you answer. "Show everything" stays one tap away.
  3. One set-up bar on every page. Gallery, build page and sheet each had their own controls (chips, a hidden colour drawer, a dropdown). Now one bar shows your couch and opens the same editor everywhere.
  4. Colours you can match by eye. Named swatches replace the system colour wheel; "Other colour" keeps the wheel. The second couch says "Same as first" until you change it. Rows appear only for pieces you own.
  5. Builds you can't make are parked, not deleted. A row says "12 more need a round add-on" and opens on tap, so nobody wonders where the castle from the video went.
  6. The build page says whether you can make it. A parent landing from a shared link sees "You have every piece" or exactly what is missing, with a pieces list.
  7. 2 couches + round add-on is now an answer. Today that family has no chip that fits them. Here it opens all 36 builds.
  8. "Something else" gets an honest answer. Three couches, another brand, add-on only: say what is not covered yet and show the closest match instead of a dead end.
  9. The child's name is asked where it is used. Optional in the editor, and again on the sheet. The sheet no longer guesses a set-up; it asks.
